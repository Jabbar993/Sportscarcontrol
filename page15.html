Welcome to the Official Spotter Guide for the TUDOR United SportsCar Championship and Continental Tires Sportscar Challenge.

The final round of the series takes place this weekend with all the championship titles still open. The guide features all four classes in TUDOR United SportsCar Championship along with the Continental Tires SportsCar Challenge.

This free download will be updated for each race  A small version of this online guide will also be available at the official site, IMSA.com, on their Event Page
